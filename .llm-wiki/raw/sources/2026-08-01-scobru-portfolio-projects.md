Active projects listed on scobru.github.io homepage portfolio:

- **Nullroute**: EVM stealth payment hub for anonymous on-chain transactions.
- **Whiterabbit**: Standalone ephemeral P2P chat.
- **Zenvault**: Decentralized secure vault for notes and secrets.
- **Zen**: Zen P2P graph engine fork.
- **Delay**: High-performance P2P relay peer.
